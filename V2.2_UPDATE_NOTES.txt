DomeSurvival V2.2 — airlock chat + custom sounds

Changes:
- Panel/command interaction now sends one concise chat status line.
- GREEN: [ШЛЮЗ] ВНЕШНЯЯ ЗАКРЫТА • КАМЕРА ПОД ДАВЛЕНИЕМ
- RED:   [ШЛЮЗ] ВНЕШНЯЯ ОТКРЫТА • КАМЕРА РАЗГЕРМЕТИЗИРОВАНА
- Interlock denial is a single YELLOW line.
- Added user-provided opening and closing sounds, converted from MP3 to Minecraft OGG/Vorbis.
- Opening any shutter plays the opening sound.
- Closing any shutter plays the closing sound.
- Closing the outer shutter now immediately restores chamber pressure.
- Legacy V2 saves with both doors closed + DEPRESSURIZED are normalized to PRESSURIZED on load.
- Structure version remains 6/6. No /dome upgrade is required.
